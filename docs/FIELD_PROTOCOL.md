# NotchCount field protocol (print this)

The estimate is only as good as the walks. Same route, same time, same pace, every dog.

## Before the first walk (Oct 20 evening)

- [ ] Pick one route of about 1 km, ideally a loop, that you can walk in 30 to 40 minutes. Lanes where dogs rest near homes and shops are best.
- [ ] Walk it once without photographing, to check it is safe and to agree the exact path and direction.
- [ ] An adult goes with every walker under 18. Tell your parents the route and time.
- [ ] Phone settings: automatic date and time on, location on at high accuracy, camera works inside the app, battery above 60%.
- [ ] Open the NotchCount link, add it to the home screen, sign in, check the route shows on the map.
- [ ] Agree the start time. Dawn works best (roughly 06:30 to 07:30): dogs are out and resting, traffic is low.

## During each pass

1. Start the pass in the app at the start point, at the agreed time (within 15 minutes on both days).
2. Walk at an even, normal pace in the agreed direction. Do not double back for a dog you missed; consistency matters more than catching every dog.
3. Photograph **every** free-roaming dog you can see from the route: sleeping, walking, puppies, ones you think you already know.
4. Two shots per dog when you can: first the whole body from the side, then the head with both ears visible. Stay at least 3 m away. Zoom instead of approaching.
5. Keep people's faces and number plates out of the frame where you can. The app blurs what it can, but avoid it anyway.
6. Do not feed, call, chase or touch dogs. Do not wake sleeping dogs for a better photo.
7. If you notice puppies or a female who is clearly nursing, take the photo as normal; the review screen lets you tag it later.
8. End the pass in the app at the finish point.

## Second pass (Oct 22)

Same route, same direction, same start time (within 15 minutes), same pace. A different team member may walk it; write who walked each pass on this sheet. If it rains heavily, skip the day and walk on the next dry morning; tell the build session so the passes stay paired.

## Safety

- If a dog approaches or growls: stop, stand still, keep your arms close, do not run or stare, back away slowly once it settles.
- If anyone is bitten or scratched: wash the wound with soap and running water for about 15 minutes, then go to a hospital or health centre the same day for anti-rabies treatment. Do not wait to see if the dog seems sick. Stop the walk.
- If a resident objects, explain politely and leave. Never argue on the street.

## Talking to people (Oct 21 to 22)

Always say who you are, what the project is, that answers are anonymous, and that they can say no. No names, no phone numbers written down.

**Households (aim for 20, with an adult present):**
1. In the last 12 months, has anyone in this home been bitten or chased by a street dog? (bitten / chased / neither)
2. If bitten: did they get anti-rabies injections? (yes / no / don't know)
3. Have you seen anyone pick up dogs for sterilisation on this lane in the last year? (yes / no / don't know)

**Interviews (10 minutes each, notes only):**
- An RWA office-bearer: complaints received, any contact with the municipal sterilisation programme.
- A feeder who knows the dogs: how many dogs they feed on the route, which ones were taken for sterilisation and returned with a notched ear. This is ground truth for checking the app's matching: ask if you may photograph the dogs they name.
- A shopkeeper on the route: changes in dog numbers, puppies seen this year.
- The municipal Animal Birth Control contact (phone or email): who is responsible for this ward, and whether coverage data is published. Get the name and email for the case file.

## Labelling session (Oct 21 evening, 60 to 90 minutes)

The build session will open two tools:
- **Pairs:** two photos side by side. Answer Same dog, Different or Not sure. Answer only what you can see; Not sure is a good answer.
- **Ears:** one head photo. Answer Notched, Not notched or Ear not visible.

Split the work so at least two people label some of the same items; the agreement rate goes in the model report.
